// HELL-MOUTH  
// SCENE 5 – MEET “THE VOICES”  
// FORMAT: SURREAL INTRO SEQUENCE / DIALOGUE + VISUAL TRANSFORMATION  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT → BLACK VOID STAGE

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

Hunter sits on the floor. Cross-legged. Ring light on.  
Face blank. Pupils dilated.

He’s mid-recording—but frozen. Not blinking.

                         HUNTER (soft, hypnotized)
          They’re not just voices.  
          They have names now.  
          Faces. Wardrobes. Intent.

He blinks—and the world shifts.

The walls dissolve.  
The LED lights melt.  
The room folds into **THE BLACK VOID STAGE**—an infinite, glowing soundstage with no exits.

                         ANNOUNCER VOICE (V.O.)
          Please welcome tonight’s panel of inner demons!

                         (echoing)
          They’re opinionated.  
          They’re chaotic.  
          And they’ve all lived rent-free in Hunter’s head since adolescence.

                         (suddenly sharp)
          Give it up for...  
          **THE VOICES.**

LIGHTS UP:

A spotlight hits **FOUR THRONES**, each lit in different colors: blood red, paparazzi flash white, soft blue, sickly gold.

Hunter stands center stage, in a faded hoodie and designer underwear.

One by one, **The Voices** appear—*manifested hallucinations of media icons Hunter has absorbed, feared, and worshipped.*

---

👠 **THE DIVA (GAGA)**  
Tall. Statuesque. Dressed in barbed wire and pearls.  
She speaks in whispered commands and music video voiceovers.

                         DIVA
          I’m the voice that tells you your suffering is *aesthetic*.

          I turned your breakdown into choreography.  
          You cried in the shower and called it cinema.

---

🧥 **THE GENIUS (YE)**  
Swaggering. Sunglasses at night. Wearing a mic like a preacher.

                         GENIUS
          I’m the voice that tells you you’re chosen.  
          That they don’t get you because you’re *ahead*.

          That the static is divine.  
          That the glitch is prophecy.

---

📰 **THE GOSSIP (HARVEY LEVIN)**  
Sweaty. Holding a tablet. Smoking a Juul.  
He looks like a TMZ tabloid headline came to life.

                         GOSSIP
          I’m the one who leaks your thoughts *before* you have them.  
          I monetize your shame in real-time.

          You tweet, I screenshot.  
          You cry, I sell ad space.

---

🎭 **THE SHOWRUNNER (RYAN MURPHY)**  
Slick. Smirking. Holding cue cards for a trauma anthology.

                         SHOWRUNNER
          I’m the one who writes your trauma into episode arcs.  
          I cast your lovers. I edit your confessions.

          Your breakdown is a series bible.  
          Your meds are just story beats.

---

Hunter looks at all of them.

                         HUNTER (dry)
          So you’re the ones writing this?

                         DIVA
          We don’t write.  
          We remix.

                         GENIUS
          You let us in.  
          Now we’re the voiceover.

                         GOSSIP
          We’ve been here since the first time you went viral in your own head.

                         SHOWRUNNER
          And we’re not leaving until the finale.

Hunter closes his eyes. Breath trembling.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I don’t know who’s real anymore.  
          But the loudest ones always sound the most like me.

He opens his eyes.  
They’re all gone.

He’s back in the apartment.  
On the floor. Still recording.

                         HUNTER
          That’s not paranoia.  
          That’s casting.

FADE OUT.

// HELL-MOUTH  
// SCENE 5 – MEET "THE VOICES"  
// FORMAT: LIVESTREAM-STYLE DIALOGUE / SURREAL REALISM  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

The ring light is ON.  
The camera is recording.  
Hunter is LIVE.

SFX: Faint reverb of stream audio in the background—on a delay, glitching.

Hunter paces in front of the couch. Shirtless. Out of breath.  
Designer water bottle in one hand, vape in the other.  
Eyes glassy, heart racing.

                         HUNTER
          Okay. Okay. Fuck it. Let’s go. I’ll say it.  
          Let’s meet the voices. Right now. Let’s do the full tour.

                         (beat)

          You think I’m playing? You think I’m making this up?

          Nah. This is episode five.  
          This is where the producers show up.

He turns to the ring light and talks into it—like it’s a person.

                         HUNTER
          There’s four of them. And they don’t shut up.

          They’re not demons. They’re not hallucinations.  
          They’re casting directors.

                         (beat)
          I call them the voices because calling them “friends” would be fucking insane.

                         (smirks)
          Too late, though, right?

He moves to the couch. Sits. Legs bouncing. Picks up a remote—turns on the massive TV behind him.

On screen: a blank Google doc titled **“Season Outline – Hunter Hardon”**

                         HUNTER
          Let’s start with the Diva. She’s the one who tells me my panic attacks are *branding.*

                         (mocking)
          “Oh my god, babe, you’re spiraling? Slay. Upload it.”

                         (beat, switches tone)
          Every time I cry she’s like, “Go live.”

                         (voice breaking)
          And I usually do.

A *ding* from the speaker system.  
A smart home assistant glitch-whispers: **“Now recording.”**

                         HUNTER
          Then there’s the Genius.

          He tells me I’m a prophet.  
          That every static buzz is a secret download.  
          That my illness is just... poor reception.

                         (leans in)
          He told me God was sending me drafts.  
          Like I’m on the cc chain of the apocalypse.

                         (laughs)
          Tell me that’s not fire though.

Hunter jumps up. Starts pacing again.

                         HUNTER
          Third one’s the Gossip.

          He leaks my thoughts before I even finish thinking them.  
          Like—

                         (snaps)
          “I’m gonna ruin my life”  
          *ding*  
          “Posted.”

                         (beat)
          He’s in the captions. In the comments.  
          In the group chat I’m not even in but somehow I know exists.

                         (softer)
          He’s the one who keeps me up at night... editing myself.

                         (stares off)
          I delete the tweet, but he saves the screenshot.

Hunter pulls his vape and hits it like it’s a religious inhaler.

                         HUNTER
          And then... there’s the Showrunner.

          He’s the one who says all this pain better lead somewhere.

          That if I’m gonna go down, it should be cinematic.  
          That relapse is a plot device.  
          That my diagnosis is a *character arc.*

                         (mockingly)
          “Big reveal, season finale. Hunter finds clarity. Cue Lana Del Rey.”

                         (angrily)
          Shut the fuck up, man.

He throws the vape across the room. It hits a velvet chair.

Silence.

                         HUNTER (quieter)
          I don’t remember inviting them in.  
          But they got the password.

                         (beat)

          I don’t know what’s mine anymore.  
          Every thought I have comes with a voiceover.

                         (to the camera)
          You ever feel like you’re the reboot of someone else’s trauma?

He breathes heavy. The red “LIVE” light reflects in his eyes.

                         HUNTER (final)
          I’m not hallucinating.  
          I’m broadcasting.

                         (smiles, wide, terrifying)
          Welcome to the stream.

FADE TO:

// HELL-MOUTH  
// CONTINUATION – “THE VOICES OF THE MANY”  
// FORMAT: STREAM-STYLE HALLUCINATION / OVERLAPPING DIALOGUE  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – SAME

Hunter’s still standing in front of the camera.  
His pupils are blown.  
His hands twitch near his mouth—he wants to say something, but something’s already saying it first.

                         HUNTER
          That’s the core four.  
          The main producers.

                         (beat)

          But then there’s... the rest of them.

                         (beat)

          The voices of the many.

A low sound begins—barely audible.  
Murmuring.  
Snippets.  
Phone notifications without phones.  
Voices that feel like comments sections, sidewalks, waiting rooms, old friends, influencers, enemies, cashiers, *you*.

They bleed in.

                         VOICE 1 (female, annoyed)
          He’s live again? Jesus. Take a nap.

                         VOICE 2 (masc, disinterested)
          Didn’t he already say this like three nights ago?

                         VOICE 3 (whispering)
          I liked him better when he was medicated.

                         VOICE 4 (ex-boyfriend?)
          He’s doing that thing where he performs clarity but he’s still fucked up.

                         VOICE 5 (young, cruel)
          Nobody even asked. Type shit.

                         HUNTER
          See?  
          They don’t shut up.

                         (beat)

          They’re not famous. Not powerful.  
          They’re just... *everyone else.*

                         (tries to laugh)
          The chorus.  
          The comment section in my skull.  
          The gallery of people who don’t matter but somehow still got cast.

The lighting dims—then sharpens.

                         VOICE 6 (echoed, nasal)
          I saw him at Erewhon once. He looked... thin.

                         VOICE 7 (older, Midwest tone)
          I mean he was always kinda off. Even as a kid.

                         VOICE 8 (therapist? parent? both?)
          He’s addicted to being witnessed. That’s the real illness.

                         VOICE 9 (deadpan, scrolling)
          He should start a podcast and stop talking.

                         VOICE 10 (mocking, queer)
          “I’m mentally ill, but make it sexy.” Type shit.

                         HUNTER
          I don’t even know which ones are real anymore.

                         (soft)
          Or maybe none of them are.

                         (beat)

          Or maybe they all are—and that’s the problem.

His eyes flutter.  
He sits on the couch again. Pulls a throw blanket over his legs like armor.

                         HUNTER (mumbling)
          They show up when I’m brushing my teeth.  
          They show up when I rewatch Euphoria like it’s church.  
          They show up when I text “I’m fine” but mean “save me.”

                         (clears throat)
          They show up in the checkout line.  
          In my emails.  
          In the *pause* before someone responds.

                         (staring off)
          Every silence is suspicious. Type shit.

FAINTLY, all the voices start whispering at once.  
Layered. Distorted. Discordant.

                         VOICES (overlapping)
          boring // too much again // is this performance? // he should go outside // should’ve stayed gone // we get it // where’s the arc?

                         HUNTER (snapping)
          Shut up. Shut up. SHUT UP.

Silence.

Then a single voice—clear, still, different.

                         STRANGE VOICE (V.O.)
          Hunter... do you know who’s watching?

He freezes.

                         HUNTER
          I don’t even know who’s *talking* anymore.

                         (soft, almost hopeful)
          But someone is. Right?

FADE OUT.
