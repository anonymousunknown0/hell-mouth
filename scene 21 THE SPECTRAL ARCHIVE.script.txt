HELL-MOUTH  
SCENE 21 — THE SPECTRAL ARCHIVE

INT. BLACK VOID – CONTINUOUS

Hunter’s body is sucked backward through the spiral.

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. ARCHIVE CHAMBER – TIMELESS

The room is massive. Empty. Gridded. Matte black.  
Every wall, floor, and ceiling divided into sterile wireframes—like a VR sandbox that forgot what it was building.

In the far distance, a single object floats:

— A DIGITAL FILE ICON.  
— Beneath it: five gray SQUARE BUTTONS.  
— Bottom left corner: A glowing red MUTE SYMBOL, pulsing softly.

Hunter stands alone.

                            HUNTER (whispering)
                  I’ve been here before...

His voice echoes—but only once. The silence is mechanical, moderated.

Then:  
A clean, almost pleasant female voice fills the room—toneless, emotionless.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  Welcome back, Hunter Hardon.
                  Please remain still while your memory loads.

Hunter steps forward. His footfalls make no sound.

He approaches the floating icon. The label reads:  
> **“LOVE_FINAL(FINAL)_REAL3”**

                            HUNTER
                  No. Not that one. I already deleted it.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  Deletion was not permanent.  
                  Your archive auto-duplicates based on emotional significance.

CLICK. The icon opens.

A giant screen stretches across the gridded wall.  
Footage begins to play:  
— Hunter on Degenerate Radio, crying.  
— Hunter telling Jack “I love you.”  
— Hunter being ignored.  
— Hunter checking his own likes mid-conversation.  
— Hunter laughing at a meme he doesn’t find funny.

Audience emojis and comment overlays glitch in:  
♥ 9.1K  
👁️‍🗨️ "so real"  
💬 "he’s spiraling again lol"

                            HUNTER (softly)
                  Turn it off.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  That is not an available action.

                            HUNTER
                  Please—

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  You wanted to be remembered.

                            HUNTER
                  Not like this.

Beat.

The screen freezes. The comment feed accelerates until it becomes noise.

Then:  
A voice begins to loop in the room, robotic, chopped and glitched:

                            VOICE (LOOPING, DISTORTED)
                  What is love?  
                  Baby don’t hurt me—  
                  Don’t hurt—  
                  Know more.  
                  Don’t hurt.  
                  Know more.  
                  Don’t hurt—  
                  (repeats)

Hunter drops to his knees.

                            HUNTER (to himself)
                  I was just trying to be understood.

The loop continues.  
The red mute symbol in the corner **glows brighter**.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  The archive is complete.  
                  Would you like to exit this memory?

Hunter doesn’t answer.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O., gentler)
                  Would you like to forget it?

                            HUNTER
                  …No.

                            ARCHIVIST (V.O.)
                  Then you may proceed.

A segment of the wall opens, revealing a staircase made of *white light*.

Hunter stands. He walks toward it.

As he steps through, the file begins auto-playing again, glitching as he leaves:

                            HUNTER (RECORDED, V.O.)
                  I love you. I love—don’t hurt—  
                  know more. Don’t—  
                  don’t—  
                  know—  
                  me.

SMASH TO:

BLACK.
