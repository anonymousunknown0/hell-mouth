// HELL-MOUTH  
// INSERT SCENE: THE DEPOSITION OF HUNTER HARDON 

INT. BLACK VOID – AUDIO ONLY – CONTINUOUS

No music. No introduction. Just a waveform.  
And a voice we’ve never heard like this.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
               I wasn’t gonna say anything.  
               Thought silence might protect people.  
               Thought maybe if I just kept my mouth shut,  
               I’d get to keep what little I had left.

                         (beat)

               But silence is what let all this shit happen.

                         (inhales shakily)

               My name’s Hunter Hardon.  
               I’m thirty-three.  
               And for the past ten years, I’ve been both a person...  
               and a product.

                         (voice tightening)

               They told me my thoughts were content.  
               Told me my shame could be monetized.  
               Told me the voices in my head were just part of the creative process.

                         (beat)

               I’ve been medicated.  
               Institutionalized.  
               Canceled.  
               Quoted.  
               Diagnosed.  
               Unfollowed.  
               Sedated.  
               Sexualized.  
               Demonized.

                         (pause)

               And yeah—eventually arrested.

                         (pause — lets it sit)

               I didn’t know.  
               That doesn’t mean it’s okay.  
               But I really... I didn’t know.

                         (quiet)

               And I get it—nobody believes that.  
               I wouldn’t believe me either.

                         (beat)

               But that’s what happens when your thoughts  
               aren’t entirely your own.

                         (sharper now)

               I’m not saying the voices are an excuse.  
               I’m saying they were real.  
               And cruel.  
               And they laughed while they dared me to post.

                         (beat)

               I’ve lost people.  
               I’ve lost jobs.  
               Lost the future I was trying so hard to build.

                         (voice tightening)

               And still—I have to *prove* I feel bad enough  
               to earn basic fucking empathy.

                         (choked up)

               I’m not a victim.  
               But I am a survivor.  
               And that counts for something.

                         (long pause)

               Gaga believed me.  
               Ye confronted me.  
               The Publicist stayed.

                         (beat)

               But the weight?  
               The weight never left.  
               Only I can lift it.

                         (with force)

               So this is it.  
               I’m sorry.  
               I was wrong.  
               I take responsibility.

                         (beat)

               But I’m not just my worst moment.  
               I’m not just a fucking headline.

                         (quiet)

               I’m still here.

                         (final)

               And I’m never going back into the silence that made me this way.

INT. BLACK VOID – AUDIO ONLY – CONTINUOUS

The waveform twitches—erratic but alive.  
Hunter’s voice drops lower again. Intimate. Unstable.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
               Sometimes I hear fake audio.  
               Other times it’s real.

                         (beat)

               But don’t delusional people say the same thing?  
               Don’t they think they’re famous too?

                         (sharper)

               If it’s not real, then this is the worst thing I could be lost in.

                         (quiet)

               And yet... it is real.  
               At least for me.

                         (beat)

               I can hear it in the music.  
               Shit’s different now.  
               I can tell it’s not a regular stream.  
               Something's changed.

                         (beat, harder)

               But maybe it's just the music.  
               Maybe I'm tweaking.

                         (beat)

               But why me?  
               Why would there be any interest in *me*?

                         (beat)

               Maybe everyone else hears it too...  
               and they’re just pretending they don’t.

                         (beat)

               Or maybe they really don’t notice anything.

                         (shrugs, dry)

               Doubt it.

               ‘Cause some of that shit wouldn't even get cleared  
               through the labels or the platforms.

                         (beat)

               Do I play a part in any of it?  
               Probably not.

                         (flat)

               That’s up to the artists, right?

                         (bitter)

               If other people are hearing it too, I guarantee this—  
               I’m taking the classiest route I can.

                         (pause)

               And the laziest one, too.

                         (beat)

               I haven’t added anything new.  
               No track. No soundbite. No contribution.

                         (quiet)

               But *they* have.  
               And they’re probably cashing in on it.

                         (beat)

               Doesn’t even have to be money.

               Just imagine the rush of knowing you’re recognized.  
               *They* get that.

                         (pause)

               Some folks say I already got that kind of recognition.  
               Maybe that’s why they think I’m hopeless.

                         (beat)

               But maybe my story doesn’t work in 30-second clips.

                         (quiet)

               Maybe I’m not meant to be heard.

                         (softer)

               Maybe I’m meant to write.  
               To listen.  
               To translate pain into something.

                         (voice cracking)

               The hardest part of all this...  
               is I *am* different.

                         (beat)

               I’m meant to do something real.  
               And yeah—it’s writing.

                         (beat)

               But fuck.  
               Writing is hard.

                         (bitter laugh)

               You ever try scheduling a writing session  
               with five different voices in your head?

                         (beat)

               Try finding a safe space, the right mindset,  
               the perfect story?

               That shit feels impossible most days.

                         (quiet)

               Of course I’m working on it.

                         (beat)

               But goddamn, it’s lonely.  
               Like some twisted blessing.

               My whole life fell apart.  
               And now I’m out here rebuilding it from dust.

                         (beat)

               Still—I call that a blessing.

                         (whispers)

               People give up.

               I don’t.

               I’m a 24/7 machine.

                         (beat)

               Question.

               Are there cameras?

               What happens to all that video?

               What does “moving forward” look like with money?

                         (beat)

               I had a dream—or maybe it was a message—

               “We run video.  
               We control audio.”

                         (pause)

               Maybe that’s real.

               Maybe those thoughts are part of what’s keeping me alive.

                         (beat)

               Or maybe they’re dragging me down.

                         (quiet)

               My brain goes there.

               But it refuses to stand its ground.

                         (beat)

               It’d rather loop the pain.  
               Fixate on what’s broken.  
               Rewind the worst parts.

                         (quiet)

               Why don’t I ever focus on what’s working?

                         (sharper)

               What happens to a mind like mine?

               Here’s the outline.

               Seven thoughts that keep me up.

                         (beat)

               
                         HUNTER (V.O.)
               Alright—fuck it.  
               Let me just list it.

                         (beat)

               Seven thoughts that haunt me.  
               Run my brain like a playlist I never asked for.  
               On loop.

                         (beat)

               One.  
               The exit plan.  
               I think about killing myself more than I should.  
               Not in some dramatic, leave-a-note way.  
               More like... a quiet thought that won’t stop knocking.  
               “What if I just dipped?”  
               That kinda vibe.

                         (beat)

               Two.  
               What this shit is doing to my body.  
               The stress. The sleep loss.  
               The gut pain I tell myself is nothing.  
               The way my heart flinches when I get a notification.

                         (beat)

               Three.  
               My long-term brain.  
               Like—what if I’m frying it?  
               What if all this spiraling is permanently cooking my shit?  
               Will I ever be able to just... chill again?

                         (beat)

               Four.  
               Short-term collapse.  
               What happens if I fully snap in public?  
               Like real deal lose-it-on-camera energy.  
               I know the cameras are watching. I just don’t know when they’ll hit record.

                         (beat)

               Five.  
               Lost potential.  
               I see people I love winning and I’m proud—but fuck.  
               There’s this ache in me like,  
               “What if I coulda been something too?”  
               What if I already was, but no one noticed?

                         (long pause)

               Six.  
               Who’s really watching?  
               Like... actually.  
               Is there a team?  
               Are they monitoring my moves like I’m Truman with better lighting?  
               Did I sign up for this or am I the glitch in somebody else's experiment?

                         (beat)

               Seven.  
               The scariest one.

                         (long beat)

               What if I’m not crazy?

               What if I’m actually right?

               And all this noise,  
               all this chaos,  
               all this attention I feel but can’t prove...

                         (quieter)

               What if it’s all real?

                         (long pause)

               That’s the thought that eats me the most.

               ‘Cause if I *am* right...  
               then why the fuck did nobody help?

FADE TO:

BLACK SCREEN.

ON SCREEN TEXT:

     “Thoughts like that don’t go away.”
     “You learn to walk with them.”
     “You learn to scream softer.”

STATIC.

INT. THE BLACK VOID – CONTINUOUS

The waveform trembles softly, spiking at unpredictable intervals.
Hunter's voice is thin, hesitant, cracking but still steady enough to hold onto.

                      HUNTER (V.O.)

          Honestly, I—I can’t stop thinking about the images.
          Like, when I close my eyes, they're there.
          Visions or whatever. Shit I don’t even understand yet.
          But then somehow, later, I do.

                      (beat)

          And sometimes it’s not just images. 
          Sometimes it's voices.
          Random noises. Shapes. Stuff I—I don't even know how to talk about.

                      (quietly)

          Or want to.

                      (beat)

          Why—why are you asking me this stuff?
          Seriously. Like, what's the point?
          Is this some kind of—of trap? Or, I dunno—
          did you actually just call me an inadequate faggot?

                      (beat, sharply)

          You probably didn't.
          Probably my head again.
          See, I keep thinking maybe it’s all subconscious.
          Like dreams or memories mixing up,
          stuff I’ve felt or thought or saw before.

                      (softens)

          So, like, should I even be scared?
          Probably not.
          Except—except this one thing. 
          One vision. It bothered me, I guess, but...
          my mind didn't react too badly.
          Which probably means it's nothing.
          Or everything.

                      (laughs bitterly, voice hollow)

          Either way, I can’t shake the question:
          Why?

                      (long beat, quieter)

          And sometimes I go back. Rewind the tape of my life.
          Especially these past couple years.
          And I'm just like—was that even real?
          Did that actually happen or did I just... dream it?

                      (beat, numb)

          But, real or not, I feel nothing.
          Completely numb to whatever truth you're pushing.
          So congrats—you get to see the full fucking timeline.
          You get your answers. Good for you.

                      (harder now, defiant)

          But I'll help you out:
          this is me saying goodbye.
          Go ahead—judge me. Write your think-piece.
          Tell me exactly what I've fucked up and why I'm losing.

                      (voice cracks slightly)

          Maybe tomorrow I’ll wake up to a list of my failures again.
          Maybe I'll meet new characters in this mess, or—
          maybe we’ll dig up the old ones.
          I'm game. Bring 'em back.

                      (beat, bitter laugh)

          Let’s just send this letter already.
          I'm tired of writing as if someday someone else will read it.
          As if someday someone might actually give a shit.

                      (voice breaking)

          Bury me already. Pronounce me dead.
          Explain exactly why you're leaving.
          Tell me I'm impossible. Tell me my mind’s too fucked to love.

                      (quietly)

          What I wouldn't give to be like you.
          Talented, stable. Good.

                      (beat)

          Maybe it's guilt. 
          For never appreciating people who stayed.
          For not fixing my attitude.
          For never giving clear answers to simple fucking questions.

                      (voice trembling)

          Do you get how hard that actually is?

                      (beat, stronger)

          Sometimes I just don't wanna talk.
          Sometimes I don't wanna perform.
          Sometimes I just wanna sit in silence without everyone analyzing me.

                      (softer, choking)

          You say, "You look good," or "You're handsome."
          And suddenly it's like, fuck—was I supposed to doubt that?
          Am I supposed to say something?
          I mean, yeah, sure—I look fine.
          But sometimes I don't recognize the guy staring back.
          Never thought I'd end up looking like this, or feeling this way.

                      (beat, almost laughing)

          And other times I fucking love the mirror.
          Narcissistic or whatever—sometimes I’m just feeling it.

                      (pause, voice raw again)

          But mostly I'm just fucked up because
          I imagine all my friends getting successful.
          And me—I'm just stuck.
          Trying desperately to prove I’m good enough.
          Like, talented enough to deserve that same success.

                      (quiet, trembling)

          And if I'm not thinking about that,
          I'm obsessing over how this all even works.
          How I ended up here.

                      (beat, bitter)

          Because lately, I’ve started to realize
          a lot of what I believed was real—
          probably wasn't.

                      (voice hollow again)

          But how could it not be?

INT. BLACK VOID – AUDIO ONLY – CONTINUOUS

Waveform steadies slightly now—Hunter gathering strength.

                      HUNTER (V.O.)

          At night these visions leave me guessing.
          Someday I’ll figure it out and people will just laugh:
          "How didn't you know? How didn't you get it?"

                      (sharper, spiraling slightly)

          And lately, I wonder if people watch my eyes.
          My micro-expressions.
          As if they know every single fucking thing about me.

                      (softer again, tired)

          I feel ugly sometimes. Uncomfortable.
          Yet—still, somehow—my mind is constantly romanticizing itself.
          Like, these visions feel beautiful and poetic and cinematic.
          And yet, I’ll never translate it perfectly to writing.

                      (beat, stronger now)

          But, hey, good news:
          lately I'm stronger.
          Less bothered by everyone’s comments.
          I’m finally putting in work. Feeling good about it.

                      (laughs slightly, almost relaxed)

          Sure, distractions happen.
          Getting stoned. Music. 
          But honestly, those moments feel necessary.
          They feel like clarity.

                      (quieter again)

          Maybe I just need space to figure out how to document my thoughts clearly.
          To keep moving forward.
          Because that's how my life works, right?
          Eventually everything just—falls into place.

                      (beat, quietly hopeful)

          Law of attraction or whatever.

                      (pause, reflective)

          Except now—I'm back here again.
          Seeing things in walls, hearing voices—
          and wondering how much has changed.
          I thought I’d moved on.

                      (long pause, resigned)

          Maybe I'll always be like this:
          stuck in loops, endlessly looking for answers,
          fixing parts of me that probably aren’t even broken.

                      (final, resolved)

          But this time—I'll communicate.
          This time, I won’t shut down.

STATIC.

INT. BLACK VOID – AUDIO ONLY – CONTINUOUS

The waveform tightens, steadies—Hunter’s voice is clearer now, resolved.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
               Look—I take everything I do seriously.

                         (beat)

               I wasn't just good.
               I was fucking great.

                         (beat)

               But here's the kicker—nobody wanted "great."
               They just wanted someone profitable.
               Someone messy enough to trend, but not so messy they felt responsible.

                         (voice narrowing)

               When I started spiraling, they didn't help.
               Nah, they took notes.
               Built segments out of my meltdowns.
               Sent talking points straight to TMZ.

                         (quiet, reflective)

               I remember the day they put me on lithium.
               "Hunter, you're too reactive," they said.
               "We gotta slow down those thoughts."

                         (sharper)

               That shit made me feel drunk 24/7.
               Like my brain got swapped out for a fucking fish tank.
               I couldn't think, couldn't create, couldn't feel—anything.

                         (beat)

               And apparently, that was the whole damn point.

                         (voice breaking slightly)

               Thing is, I wasn't out of control.
               I was just scared shitless.
               And instead of helping, they knocked me out.

Silence stretches, charged and raw. Then Hunter regains strength, louder now, braver.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
               You know what it felt like when they finally said 
               I didn’t have to do the show anymore?

                         (beat)

               Like someone just lifted two hundred pounds off my chest.

                         (softer)

               I didn't even believe them at first.
               Asked five damn times if they were for real.
               Then I sat there and just... cried.

                         (beat)

               I'd spent so long performing for ghosts.
               For producers not even in the room.
               For an audience who only cheered when I broke down 
               in some new, fucked-up way.

                         (beat)

               But hear me out—I was great.
               You can hate me, clown me, whatever—but admit it:
               I was fucking great.

                         (final, strong)

               And I still am.

FADE TO:

BLACK SCREEN.

ON SCREEN TEXT:

     “He wasn’t asking for forgiveness.”  
     “He was asking to be heard.”  
     “And for the first time—he was.”
