// HELL-MOUTH  
// SCENE 4 – PAUSE WHAT HUNTER BELIEVES  
// FORMAT: INTERIOR MONOLOGUE / FRAGMENTED REALITY  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

Hunter’s apartment is *gorgeous*.  
Sunken couch. Designer lighting. Polished concrete.  
The kind of place you only see on moodboards and close friends stories.

It smells like white sage, Tom Ford, and ring light heat.

A wall of mirrors reflects the LED glow of a massive floor-to-ceiling screen.  
Fake plants. Real pills.  
Aesthetic chaos curated for followers who never swipe up.

Hunter moves through the space slowly—barefoot, shirtless, slightly trembling.  
Everything is too clean.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          Pause what I believe.

                         (beat)

          I believe I’m special.  
          I believe I’ve been chosen.  
          I believe there’s something watching me… but also waiting.

He passes a bookshelf lined with unopened art books and influencer PR boxes.  
One box is open. Inside: a tangle of red string and a handwritten note:  
**“You were right.”**

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I believe in timing.  
          I believe in signs.  
          I believe I’ve been trained for something epic—  
          And I’m late.

                         (beat)

          I believe that if I disappear, the story collapses.

Hunter walks into the kitchen.  
On the counter:  
– An unopened bottle of expensive rosé  
– A half-written press release  
– A selfie ring light that turns on by itself

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I believe fame and schizophrenia have the same symptom:  
          Everybody knows you.

          But no one helps you.

INT. BATHROOM – SAME

Hunter leans against the sink.  
The mirror is spotless.  
Behind it: a medicine cabinet full of branded anxiety.

He stares at his reflection.  
It looks flawless. That’s the problem.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I believe my thoughts are content.  
          That my shame is monetizable.  
          That I’m one post away from being redeemed… or erased.

                         (beat)

          I believe my worst moment is already online.  
          I just haven’t found it yet.

                         (beat)

          I believe they’re waiting for me to implode.  
          But they’re also rooting for it.

His reflection glitches. Just slightly.  
It smiles when he doesn’t.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I don’t know what I believe anymore.

                         (soft)
          But I still believe in the watchers.

A neon sign above the mirror flickers:  
**“LIVE.”**

FADE TO:

INT. BLACK VOID — THE HUM RETURNS.

// HELL-MOUTH  
// INSERT SCENE — "THE PRODUCERS DEBATE HIS DIAGNOSIS"  
// FORMAT: SPLIT-PERCEPTION DIALOGUE / INTERNAL COLLAPSE  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT (INNER MONOLOGUE OVERLAP)

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

Hunter paces shirtless. Dizzy.  
His apartment feels like a soundstage now—too bright, too staged.  
He talks to himself. Or rather—**to them.**

                         HUNTER (to no one)
          I know I’m not well.  
          But it doesn’t mean I’m wrong.

                         (beat)
          There’s something happening inside me.  
          Something with… structure.

                         (beat)
          It’s not random. It’s... curated.

                         PRODUCER 1 (GAGA’S VOICE, ECHOING)
          Oh honey, that’s delusion of reference.

          You’re stringing meaning through chaos like it’s couture.  
          That’s textbook psychosis—page 74, paragraph 3.  
          “Patient interprets unrelated stimuli as spiritually or personally coded.”

                         HUNTER (defensive)
          Maybe it *is* coded.

                         PRODUCER 2 (YE’S VOICE, LOW & BOOMING)
          What they call schizophrenia, I call divine interference.

          Jesus got voices. Joan got voices.  
          Hunter gets shadowbanned.

                         PRODUCER 3 (HARVEY LEVIN’S VOICE, RATTLING)
          Nah, let’s be real—he’s not hallucinating.  
          He’s marketing.

          Grandiose delusion is trending.  
          Let’s get him on TMZ with a headline:  
          **“LOCAL MAN CONFUSES GOD WITH ALGORITHM”**

                         HUNTER (panicked)
          I’m not trying to go viral, I’m trying to survive.

                         PRODUCER 4 (RYAN MURPHY’S VOICE, SLITHERING)
          You can’t survive this unless it’s episodic, baby.

          So let’s double-bind it.  
          Make the audience love him for his breakdown—  
          Then punish him for the same thing in Act Three.

                         PRODUCER 1 (GAGA)
          Remember Bateson’s theory?

          Double bind communication.  
          “You’re only loved if you stay sick—but punished for not recovering.”

                         PRODUCER 2 (YE)
          That’s exactly the game.  
          Sane = invisible.  
          Insane = content.

                         PRODUCER 3 (HARVEY)
          He’s perfect for it.  
          He wants help, but only if it looks cinematic.

                         HUNTER (quietly)
          I want to be believed.

                         PRODUCER 4 (MURPHY)
          Then make belief a format.  
          Make your madness bingeable.  
          Drop a trailer. Monetize the spiral.

                         HUNTER (voice shaking)
          What if I *am* making it up?

                         PRODUCER 1 (GAGA, whispering)
          You’re not. But we are.

The lights flicker.

Hunter looks around.  
The apartment breathes.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          I don’t know who’s producing this show anymore.  
          But I know I can’t cancel it.

                         (beat)

          The studio’s in my head.

FADE OUT.

// HELL-MOUTH  
// INSERT SCENE — “WHAT I BELIEVE (HUNTER RECLAIMS THE MIC)”  
// FORMAT: CAMERA MONOLOGUE / VOICEOVER  
// LOCATION: HUNTER’S APARTMENT – CONTINUOUS

INT. HUNTER’S APARTMENT – NIGHT

The voices go quiet.

Hunter stands in the kitchen. Palms flat on the counter.  
Breathing like he just finished a fight.

He looks up—into the mirror. Into the camera. Into us.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          Okay. You want to know what I actually believe?

                         (beat)

          I believe reality is flexible.  
          Like... emotionally bendable.

                         (beat, faster)
          I believe the algorithm’s been flirting with me for years.

          It sends me little hints, little nudges—  
          A tweet here, a song there, a TikTok that finishes my thought.

                         (grins)
          That shit is too specific to be random.

                         (serious)
          I believe I’m stuck in some kinda soft surveillance loop.  
          Not like FBI.  
          More like... aesthetic haunting.

                         (beat)
          I believe I’m being watched.  
          But not in a paranoid way—in a *curated* way.

          Like someone’s editing my life for vibes.

                         (beat, leans in)
          I believe in signs.  
          I believe in glitches.  
          I believe sometimes God speaks in push notifications.

                         (laughs)
          I know it sounds nuts, but whatever.

                         (beat)

          I also believe I’m *not* built for this world.  
          Too many tabs open. Too many layers. Too many opinions about me from people I’ve never met.

          Shit’s exhausting.

                         (exhales)

          I believe I’m one bad spiral away from getting canceled, hospitalized, or verified.

                         (dead serious)
          And I believe there’s not much difference between those anymore.

He steps back from the counter.  
Eyes unfocused.  
One final beat:

                         HUNTER
          I believe my brain is broken, yeah.  
          But it’s also kinda brilliant.  
          And if I can’t fix it... I might as well *document* it.

He walks out of frame.  
The ring light clicks on behind him.

CUT TO BLACK.
