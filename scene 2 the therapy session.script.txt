INT. THERAPY ROOM – DAY

Silence.  
The hum of white noise.  
Hunter looks down, thumb pressing into his knee.

Then—  
For the first time—  
The therapist speaks.

                         THERAPIST (soft, deliberate)
          There’s a danger in being too self-aware, Hunter.

                         (beat)

          Especially for someone like you.

Hunter looks up, confused. Defensive. Not angry—just raw.

                         HUNTER
          What does that mean?

                         THERAPIST
          It means you're narrating your illness like a screenplay—
          But you’re still inside it.

                         (beat)

          You’ve turned your psychosis into a story.

          And the better you tell it...  
          The harder it is to escape.

Hunter’s lips part slightly. He blinks like he’s buffering.

                         THERAPIST
          Insight doesn’t always mean safety.

          Sometimes it just gives the delusion a better costume.

Hunter tries to laugh—but doesn’t. Just exhales.

                         HUNTER
          So what, I stop understanding myself?

                         THERAPIST
          No.

                         (leans in)
          But maybe... you stop trying to *win* the narrative.

                         (quiet)
          And start surviving it.

Hunter stares. A flicker of something ancient in his face.  
Childlike. Terrified. Seen.

CUT TO BLACK.

INT. THERAPY ROOM – DAY

Hunter is slouched in a chair that tries too hard to be comfortable.

He’s holding a stress ball but not using it.  
He stares just past the therapist—like he’s trying to read their mind before they speak.

                         HUNTER
          So... lately I’ve been feeling like I’m being watched.

                         (beat)

          Not by you.  
          Not even by people, really.

          By something... ambient.  
          Like there’s a livestream of my inner monologue, and I just don’t know what platform it's on.

He looks down, embarrassed. Then decides to lean in.

                         HUNTER
          It’s not a delusion of grandeur thing.
          I’m not saying I’m Kanye.

                         (beat)

          I’m saying sometimes it feels like I’m *on*.

          Like I’ll think something—some dumb thought like,  
          “I want a blueberry smoothie”—
          and then someone on TikTok will post about it thirty seconds later.

                         (laughs nervously)
          Coincidence, right?

          But it doesn’t feel like coincidence.  
          It feels like the algorithm knows me *too* well.

          Or maybe... I *am* the algorithm.

A buzz from a fluorescent light above. His eyes twitch toward it—then back.

                         HUNTER
          You ever heard of thought broadcasting?

                         (beat)

          I googled it, so you don’t have to write it down.

                         (beat)

          It’s this thing where you believe your thoughts are leaking—
          like your brain’s been hacked and left unsecured.

          That’s what it feels like.  
          Not like I’m famous, but like I’m... *transmitted.*

          Like I’m on a psychic stage, and the audience is made of people I’ve never met.

          And they’re judging me.  
          Or worse—curating me.

He grips the stress ball now. Squeezes it until it squeaks.

                         HUNTER
          I can’t tell if I’m performing anymore.  
          Or if I’ve just fully become the character they want.

                         (beat)

          My therapist before you told me it was delusions of reference.

          Like when I hear a line in a show and I *know* it’s about me.

                         (scoffs)
          But it’s not narcissism.

          It’s not that I want to be the center of the universe.
          It’s that I can’t tell where I end and the world begins.

Hunter stops. Closes his eyes.

                         HUNTER
          It’s exhausting.

                         (soft)
          It’s lonely as hell.

He opens his eyes again. Looks directly into the camera, into us, into the therapist.

                         HUNTER
          I don’t want to be famous anymore.  
          Even fake-famous.

          I just want one thought...  
          that no one else hears.

A silence. A deep one.  

In the background, a faint *ding*—like a notification—echoes even though no devices are visible.

FADE TO BLACK.

INT. THERAPY ROOM – DAY

Hunter sits forward this time. Not slouched. Not relaxed.  
He wants to get this out before he backs out.

The room is soft and still. A white-noise machine hums like a fake womb.

                         HUNTER
          Okay. I’m gonna say the thing.

                         (beat)

          Yes. It’s grandiose delusion.

                         (beat)

          I know that.

          I know I’m not actually famous.  
          There’s no fanbase. No secret cameras.  
          No underground forum decoding my outfits.

                         (smirks, quiet)
          I checked.

                         (beat, honest)
          But I *feel* famous.

          Not “celebrity” famous.  
          Myth famous.  
          Like a fallen angel in a teen Netflix show.

He leans forward, hands between his knees.

                         HUNTER
          The thing is… it’s not even fun.  
          It’s not me strutting through life like a diva.

          It’s pressure.  
          Constant exposure.  
          Like I’m being recorded for a show no one asked for and everyone’s reviewing in real time.

                         (beat)

          I know it’s delusional.  
          I can clock it. I can name it.  
          “Grandiose.” “Narcissistic.” “Unfounded.”

          But knowing doesn’t *cure* it.

                         (voice rising slightly)
          Knowing just makes it worse.

                         (beat)

          Because then it’s not just delusion.  
          It’s shame wrapped around delusion like bacon on a fucking jalapeño popper.

                         (off therapist’s stare)
          Sorry. That was dumb. I’m hungry.

Hunter looks down. His fingernails are chewed.

                         HUNTER
          I have this thought—like if I just said the *right* thing, in the *right* moment, in the *right* tone…

          I’d go viral.  
          Not online.  
          Spiritually.

          Like something would *unlock* in the universe.  
          And everyone would finally say:
          “Oh my god. That’s him.”

                         (smiles sadly)
          I want to be recognized.  
          Not liked. Not even loved.  
          Just... seen.

                         (beat)

          And yeah, that’s probably a trauma thing.  
          But it’s also a fucking *me* thing.

                         (quieter)
          And if I let go of it...  
          If I stop believing I’m destined for something?

                         (beat)

          Then I have to live this regular life.  
          In this regular body.  
          With this brain that leaks.

He swallows. Looks up at the therapist. Eyes wet, but steady.

                         HUNTER
          So yeah. It’s a grandiose delusion.

                         (beat)

          But sometimes it’s the only thing holding me together.

A long silence. White noise swells slightly.

Hunter’s eyes flick to the corner of the ceiling—checking for a camera.

CUT TO BLACK.

INT. THERAPY ROOM – STILL FRAME – TIMELESS

The therapist is frozen. Unmoving.  
Hunter doesn’t blink.

The air feels pressurized.

Then, slowly—  
Hunter begins.

                         HUNTER (calm, flat)
          If I disappeared, would anyone care—or would they celebrate?

                         (beat)

          What if I fake a hate crime for attention.

                         (building)
          I could ruin someone’s life with one post.

                         (sharper)
          He only loves me because I’m broken.

                         (whispers)
          I should’ve let them overdose.

                         (suddenly brutal)
          I hope she crashes. Just to prove I’m psychic.

                         (shrugs)
          I miss being abused. At least I had a role.

                         (matter-of-fact)
          What if I’m the villain in everyone’s story?

                         (voice tightening)
          I think I like it when they’re scared of me.

                         (slow, almost proud)
          If I died right now, it would trend.

The lighting shifts slightly. Something glitches in the air.

                         HUNTER (deadpan)
          He’s too stupid to deserve empathy.

          I could stab him with this pen.

          God watches me masturbate. And He likes it.

                         (dry laugh)
          The baby cried too long. I fantasized about silence.

                         (aggressive)
          What if I said the most cancelable thing I could think of—right now?

                         (leaning in)
          I’m the only real person here. Everyone else is scripted.

                         (rushed, unraveling)
          Every time someone’s happy, it feels like a personal attack.

          I hope my ex gets sick—terminal sick.

          I wanted him to hit me so I could post it.

                         (inhales)
          I’m not sorry. I’m just sorry I got caught.

                         (smiling, sharp)
          I could make people kill themselves if I wanted.

                         (pauses, ashamed)
          Maybe I don’t want to get better.

                         (accusatory)
          Therapy is just emotional porn.

                         (almost sexy)
          If I relapsed, everyone would finally pay attention.

                         (suddenly seductive)
          I want him to fall in love with me just so I can destroy him.

                         (monotone)
          I smile in public but I imagine setting buildings on fire.

          I only donate to be seen donating.

                         (faster, twitchy)
          I think I liked it when they called me a slur.

          Sometimes I want to scream “bomb” in a crowded room—just to feel power.

                         (pauses)
          I don’t think I believe in morality. Just optics.

                         (beat)
          Her child died and I still think she’s annoying.

                         (tilts head, calm)
          My love is a weapon. I want them to bleed.

          I fantasize about school shootings and I’m not in school.

          I want to ruin someone just to feel alive.

                         (soft, eerie)
          Every apology I’ve ever made was calculated.

                         (laughs once, bitter)
          What if I told everyone I had cancer—just to reset my reputation?

                         (beat)
          I want to be hated by the entire internet. That would feel real.

                         (mock sweetness)
          I only support movements that benefit my image.

                         (serious)
          I could seduce a priest just to corrupt him.

                         (whispers)
          I dream of being assassinated on live TV.

          She’s grieving, but her tears look fake.

                         (faint smile)
          I want to make a scene at someone else’s funeral.

          I google “missing persons” and imagine they were mine.

                         (beat)
          If you knew what I really think about people, you'd never follow me.

                         (fragile)
          Part of me liked the fame that came with my breakdown.

                         (beat)
          I think everyone deserves at least one beautiful tragedy.

                         (eyes wide)
          Sometimes I imagine my friends choking on their own secrets.

                         (final blow)
          What if this entire story ends with me smiling in court?

Hunter stops.  
No music.  
No breath.

Just the buzz of the lights overhead.  
The therapist hasn’t moved.

                         HUNTER (quiet)
          Those are just some of them.

                         (beat)
          The ones that still let me sleep.

FADE TO BLACK.
