// HELLMOUTH
// SCENE 1 - SCHIZOPHRENIA DISCLAIMER

INT. BLACK VOID – DIRECT TO CAMERA – TIMELESS

HUNTER HARDON sits in a black chair.

Face lit by a single overhead light.  
Eyes tired. But sharp.  
A flicker of mischief under the exhaustion.

His breathing is audible. No music. No edits.

                         HUNTER (to camera)
          Alright, let’s get this part out the way.

                         (beat)

          This project contains symptoms of schizophrenia.
          Not the textbook ones. The real ones.

          Thought loops.  
          Audio hallucinations.  
          Conspiracies that feel... cinematic.

                         (leans in, serious)
          I used to think I was famous.
          Not like TikTok-famous.
          I mean like—chosen. Prophesied.  
          Like every song on the radio was about me.

                         (beat)

          Turns out, I was just very sick.  
          And very online.

                         (beat, shrugs)
          I know. Plot twist.

A faint spiral pulses behind him—soft, like a screensaver.

                         HUNTER (to camera)
          Look—this isn’t a PSA.  
          I’m not trying to be your after-school special.

          But also, don’t get it twisted—
          This isn’t a joke, either.

          This is my brain.
          Or... what's left of it.
          
          Presented in surround sound.

                         (voice cracks slightly)
          If it gets too real—pause it.  
          Or don’t.

          I can’t stop it either.

He stares into the lens.

The SPIRAL flickers again—red this time. Faster.

                         HUNTER (to camera)
          Let’s begin.

          Just remember:  
          If you hear something that sounds like your own thoughts...

                         (smiles, almost kind)
          That just means the signal found you.

CUT TO BLACK.

SMASH TITLE:

**HELL-MOUTH**
