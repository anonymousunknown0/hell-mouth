// FORMAT: SCREENPLAY – SCRIPTED VOICEOVER / MONTAGE

BLACK SCREEN.

The gentle crackle of radio static.  
A glitched, haunted voice—half narrator, half something else—breaks the silence:

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          You want to know his name?
          You want numbers? Data?
          Fine. Let’s do this by the book.

CUT TO:

EXT. CITY STREET – MORNING

TIMELAPSE —  
Pedestrians flow around HUNTER HARDON, who stands perfectly still, almost spectral.

ON-SCREEN LOWER THIRD (clinical, deadpan):

          HUNTER HARDON  
          Age: 33  
          Height: 6'1"  
          Weight: 174 lbs  
          Gender Identity: Male (fluid presentation)  
          Sexual Identity: Queer  
          Diagnosis: Schizophrenia-spectrum, Generalized Anxiety, Major Depression  
          Medications: Adderall, Abilify  
          Abilities: Shape-shifting, Disappearing, Dissociating, Hallucinating  
          Vulnerabilities: Attention, Addiction, Abandonment, The Internet

A slow blink.  
SOUND: The city hushes. Everything muffles and detaches.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          Meet Hunter Hardon.
          Not his birth name.
          Not his drag name.
          Just the name he made up the day reality tapped out.

INSERT POV:  
People pass by, indifferent.  
Hunter is an island.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          Schizophrenia is one of those words that scares people.

          It sounds like a prison sentence. Or a punchline.

          The truth is… it’s a spectrum.

          It’s confusion, sometimes.  
          It’s feeling like you’re not inside your own life.  
          It’s believing in signals, patterns, secret meanings—  
          Even when everyone else just calls it noise.

FLASH INSERTS:

— Hunter studying his own eyes in a mirror, face flickering.  
— A celebrity breakdown montage on his cracked phone screen.  
— A subway ad: “ARE YOU REAL?” next to a perfume bottle.  
— A TikTok voiceover: “POV: you’ve been watched your entire life.”

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          They say I have hallucinations. Auditory, mostly.

          Sometimes I hear voices. Or I think I do.

          Sometimes I feel like I’m being recorded.  
          Followed. By strangers. By thoughts. By something bigger.

          But here’s the thing.

          When you’re famous—or when people *think* you are—  
          Every day is like living inside a rumor.

          Everything’s amplified. Everything’s loaded.

          You start to wonder if the diagnosis is just...  
          a new way of being public.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          The doctors tried to chart him.  
          The police tried to book him.  
          The city tried to scroll past.

          But here he is.

CUT TO:

INT. BATHROOM – DAY

Hunter, towel loose, stares into a cracked mirror.  
He studies his reflection like it's an enemy.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          The line between delusion and reality?

          I wish I could tell you where it is.

          Is it paranoia, if people really *are* watching?

          Is it a hallucination, if you’re trending on TikTok?

          Am I sick, or am I just... visible?

          Or maybe it’s all the same thing now.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          The mindless mass—bodies colliding, hearts locked up, eyes empty.

          You want to know the difference between my mindlessness and theirs?

          Mine is curated. Performed. A televised event.

CUT TO:

EXT. CITY STREET – CONTINUOUS

Hunter walks against the tide of the crowd.  
Everyone else is blurred.  
He sees too much.

                         HUNTER (V.O.)
          They want to know what’s wrong with me.

          Sometimes I want to know, too.

          Sometimes I wish I could just turn it off—  
          The thoughts. The watching. The static.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          Mindlessness isn’t an illness.

          It’s an epidemic.

CUT TO:

EXT. ALLEYWAY – MAGIC HOUR

Hunter pauses in a puddle of neon.  
He looks over his shoulder, exposed. Vulnerable.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          What makes him different?

          He knows he’s being watched.

CLOSE-UP:  
Hunter opens his mouth—wants to confess.  
Only breath comes out.

He turns.  
Walks into the crowd.  
Vanishes.

                         NARRATOR (V.O.)
          Are you still watching?

                         (beat)

          Good.

          You’ll want to see what happens next.

SMASH TO BLACK.
