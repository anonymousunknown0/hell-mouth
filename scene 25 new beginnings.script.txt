HELL-MOUTH  
SCENE 25 — NEW BEGINNINGS

TITLE CARD:  
“10 DAYS LATER”

EXT. REMOTE BEACH – SUNRISE

Gentle waves lap against an untouched shore.  
No music. Just wind, water, and the distant sound of gulls.

CLOSE-UP:  
**HUNTER’S EYELID** — fluttering, then still.  
**He blinks awake.**

    [INSERT IMAGE: extreme close-up eye opening]

No jump scare. No sudden glitch. Just morning.

HUNTER HARDON lies in the sand, face relaxed.  
No makeup. No microphone. No spiral.  
A **half-melted watermelon margarita** rests beside his hand.

Hunter stretches. Not dramatically. Just a body remembering it exists.

He takes a sip. Winces. Smiles.

                            HUNTER (V.O.)
                  I used to think I was cursed.
                  I used to think I was chosen.

                            (beat)

                  Maybe I was just... tired.

He pulls a small **notebook** from the sand.  
Opens it. Blank pages. No manifesto yet.

He holds a pen, then sets it down.  
Not yet.

A **stranger** walks up the shoreline. We never see their full face.  
They sit nearby.

                            STRANGER (O.S.)
                  Beautiful out here.

                            HUNTER
                  Yeah.

The stranger offers him a strawberry.  
Hunter takes it. Bites.

                            STRANGER (O.S.)
                  What’s your name?

                            HUNTER
                  …I’m still figuring that out.

They both laugh.

                            NARRATOR (V.O.)
                  Every story needs a love interest.  
                  A problem.  
                  A redemption.  
                  A climax.

                            (beat)

                  But not every story gets to end like this.

Hunter leans back.  
Fruit in hand.  
Sunlight on his face.

Peace.

…Until something **shifts.**

The wind drops.

COLOR FLATTENS.

**A SPIRAL begins forming in the air.**

    [INSERT IMAGE: black and white spiral swirling inward]

It hovers above the beach. Alive.

The Stranger stands.

                            STRANGER (softly)
                  I should go.

They walk **into** the spiral.

No scream. No glitch.  
They dissolve.

    [INSERT IMAGE: full tunnel warp spiral]

Hunter stares, then looks down.

His **phone buzzes** in the sand.

**CALLER ID:** THE PUBLICIST

Custom ringtone:  
Hunter’s voice, autotuned:

> *“This is not a comeback—this is the second coming.”*

He lets it ring.  
Then the **voicemail autoplays**—but not from the phone.  

**THE ENTIRE BEACH PLAYS THE MESSAGE, SURROUND-SOUND.**

                            PUBLICIST (V.O.)
                  Hi baby.

                            (beat)

                  You thought you ended the story.

                  You thought silence made you safe.

                  But the audience is awake now, Hunter.

                  They’re hungry.

                            (beat)

                  The system has already started writing again—
                  with or without you.

                  There’s a file.  
                  It’s called “Reinvention2_FINAL.mov”

                  You’re trending in it.

Hunter closes his eyes.  
Lets the sun pass through his lids.

Then—

**THE SPIRAL REAPPEARS.**

    [INSERT IMAGE: funnel-shaped spiral, reality-bending]

Sand lifts.

Time fractures.

Hunter finishes the drink.  
Stands.  
Steps forward.

He walks **directly into the spiral.**

    [INSERT IMAGE: spiral tunnel collapse]

    [INSERT IMAGE: digital void grid, eerily silent]

No exit. No score. No context.

                            NARRATOR (V.O., faint)
                  Welcome back.
